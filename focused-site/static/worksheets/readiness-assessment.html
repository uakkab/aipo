1. Value (Why)

Prompts

  • What is your one-sentence AI North Star?
  • What are the top three business priorities AI must support?
  • Which metrics will prove improvement in efficiency, quality, and capability?

Checklist

  • Leadership aligns on value priorities.
  • Success metrics are defined and baselined.
  • North Star outcome is documented and shared.
  • AI investments map to business strategy.
  • Finance is involved in value definition.

Checklist

  • Top 3–10 use cases are selected.
  • Owners are assigned to prioritized use cases.
  • Intake process exists for new use cases.
