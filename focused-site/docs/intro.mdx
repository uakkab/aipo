# Overview

This playbook helps teams move from AI experimentation to repeatable, governed business outcomes. It is designed for leaders, delivery teams, and process owners who need a practical transformation path.

## What Is an AI+ Organization?

An AI+ organization treats AI as an operating capability, not just a tool. AI is embedded into decision-making, delivery workflows, and continuous improvement loops.

## How To Use This Site

Read first. Implement second.

1. **Read in order** through Foundation and Playbook. Stay on the pages and use the sidebar Next / Previous controls. Avoid jumping into checklists or assessments while you are still building understanding.
2. **Then open Implementation.** The Implementation Guide walks you step by step from value through execution.
3. **Use Reference when you need tools**—printable readiness checks, assessment worksheets, domain deep dives, and external standards. Those belong to facilitation and execution, not to the first reading pass.

## Outcomes You Should Expect

- Clear AI value targets linked to business outcomes
- Prioritized use case portfolio with ownership
- Defined human-AI operating modes and controls
- Stronger governance for security, privacy, and trust
- Measurable improvements in efficiency, quality, and capability
