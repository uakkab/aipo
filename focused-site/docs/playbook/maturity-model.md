# Maturity Model

Identify your current state and define the next practical upgrade. This page is part of the reading pass—use it to understand levels before you implement.

## Level Summary

| Level | Name | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Adoption | Prove value with controlled experiments |
| 2 | Integration | Standardize and scale repeatable delivery |
| 3 | Transformation | Redesign value streams around AI capabilities |

## Capability Matrix

| Dimension | Level 1: Adoption | Level 2: Integration | Level 3: Transformation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy | Tactical pilots | Aligned roadmap | AI central to enterprise strategy |
| Use Cases | Isolated wins | Portfolio-managed | End-to-end value stream redesign |
| Operating Model | Team-specific | Standard roles and workflows | AI-native operating model |
| Data and Technology | Fragmented baseline | Trusted shared platform | Real-time, composable ecosystem |
| Governance | Reactive controls | Defined governance framework | Proactive adaptive governance |
| People and Culture | Early champions | Broad AI literacy and adoption | Continuous innovation culture |
| Measurement | Pilot KPIs only | KPI tracking across portfolio | Continuous optimization loops |

## Exit Criteria Per Level

### Move from Level 1 to Level 2

- At least three use cases delivering measured value
- Common delivery pattern documented and reused
- Governance checks consistently applied before production

### Move from Level 2 to Level 3

- AI embedded in core workflows across multiple functions
- Quantitative improvement loop operating at portfolio level
- Business model or customer experience materially redesigned

## Typical Risks by Level

| Level | Common Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pilot sprawl with no scale path | Enforce portfolio prioritization and owners |
| 2 | Process overhead slows innovation | Tailor control depth by risk tier |
| 3 | Automation outpaces governance | Strengthen monitoring and human override design |

## CMMI Mapping

- Level 1 (Adoption) aligns mostly to controlled discovery from early CMMI behaviors.
- Level 2 (Integration) aligns to managed and defined process capability.
- Level 3 (Transformation) aligns to quantitatively managed and optimizing behaviors.

Maturity is not linear for every team, but governance and measurement must mature before autonomy increases.
